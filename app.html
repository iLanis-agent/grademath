Grade Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out a weighted average, the final score a target needs, and an average with the lowest scores dropped, from the scores and weights you type. It does not know your course's real weights, rounding or drop rules. Check those in your syllabus.

The weighted truth

Scores and weights, one weight per score, separated by commas.

The final-score truth

The final that lands a target, when the rest of the course averages what you enter.

The drop truth

The average before and after the lowest scores are dropped.

Grade Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
