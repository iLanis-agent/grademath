Grade Math

A weighted average is not the plain average.

Boundary: arithmetic only Grade Math works out a weighted average, the final score a target needs, and an average with the lowest scores dropped, from the scores and weights you type. It does not know your course's real weights, rounding or drop rules. Check those in your syllabus.

The weighted truth

Scores 90, 80 and 70 with weights 50, 30 and 20 give a weighted average of 83. The plain average is 80.

The final-score truth

With 85 on the other 60% and the final worth 40%, a final of 97.5 gives 90.

The drop truth

Dropping the lowest score of 4: average goes from 82.5 to 90.

Run your numbers
Grade Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
